6. Your rights

You can ask us, free of charge, to:

  • tell you what data we hold and how we use it, and give you a copy;
  • correct, update or complete it;
  • delete it or stop processing it, where the law allows;
  • restrict (block) it while a question about it is resolved;
  • give you the data you provided in a portable format;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, as easily as you gave it.

Most details can be changed directly in My account. For anything else write to [privacy@pupbox.ge]. We answer within 10 working days (in exceptional cases up to 10 more, and we will tell you why). We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

If you think we have handled your data unlawfully you may complain to the State Audit Office of Georgia (personal data protection supervision) [confirm current supervisory authority and contact] or to a court.

7. Browser storage — no tracking cookies

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. The site keeps a few items in your browser’s local storage that it needs to work:

  • pupbox_token — keeps you signed in;
  • pupbox_session — remembers your cart before you sign in;
  • pupbox_lang — your language;
  • pupbox_dismissed_popups, pupbox_popups_shown_today — so an offer you closed doesn’t keep reappearing.

Clearing your browser data removes them. If we ever add analytics or advertising tools we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

8. Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

9. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), passwords stored only as one-way hashes, access limited to staff who need it and regular updates. If a breach could seriously affect you, we will tell you without undue delay and notify the supervisory authority as the law requires.

10. Changes

We will post any change here with a new date, and tell you by email before significant changes take effect.
